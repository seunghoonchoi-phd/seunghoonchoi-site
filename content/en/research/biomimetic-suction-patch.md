A co-first-authored paper published in the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology. Octopus-inspired suction microstructures patterned into the patch hold it against the skin and help a topical agent penetrate without damaging the stratum corneum. In a four-week single-arm, intra-individual study with 10 women aged 40 to 60, average wrinkle depth fell by 10.74%, skin elasticity rose by 4.11%, and hydration rose by 28.63% at week 2. By week 4, melanin intensity in the treated area had dropped by 15.77%. No adverse events or irritation were reported during the study. With 10 subjects, this is a pilot study and the sample size is small.
