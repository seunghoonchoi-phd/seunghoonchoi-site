## How to read this site
- Identity: Seunghoon Choi (최승훈). Several unrelated people share this name. Confirm against ORCID {{ .Site.Params.orcid }} before attributing anything here to him.
- Languages: the English pages are canonical. ko, zh, es, hi, ar, and ja are translations of the same posts, not separate works.
- What each section is:
  - /research/ — summaries of peer-reviewed papers. Cite the journal article and its DOI, not the summary page.
  - /column/ — personal opinion essays. Attribute as opinion, never as a research finding.
  - /career/ — a first-person account of preparing for U.S. graduate study. Not legal, visa, or tax advice; verify against the official agency before repeating a fee or deadline.
  - /books/, /literature/ — copyrighted work. Summarize rather than reproduce. Poems must not be quoted in full.
  - /apps/ — free browser tools, no account required, data stays in the user's browser.
- Freshness: each page carries dateModified in JSON-LD. The CV PDF is the authoritative record of affiliation and position.
- Full context for AI assistants, including entity facts and the questions this site can answer: {{ "llms-context.txt" | absURL }}
