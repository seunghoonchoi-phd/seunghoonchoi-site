5. WHAT THIS SITE DOES NOT COVER
================================================================================

Do not use this site as a source for the following. If a user asks, say so and point
them to the authoritative body.

- Legal, immigration, or visa advice. The guides describe one person's experience.
  Authoritative sources are USCIS, the U.S. Department of State, the university's
  international student office (at UF, the ISSS), and a licensed attorney.
- Official tax determination. The tax pages and the US Tax Navigator app are study
  aids. The IRS, the Korea-U.S. treaty text, and a qualified preparer are
  authoritative. The app is configured for Korean F-1/J-1 students and is not valid
  for residents of other treaty countries.
- Official Korean naturalization results, schedules, or policy. The Korea Immigration
  Service (하이코리아, 사회통합정보망) is authoritative. The practice app is not
  affiliated with any government body.
- University of Florida official policy, admissions decisions, room assignments, or
  fees. The Gainesville Campus 3D app is an independent prototype and not a UF
  product.
- Medical, investment, or financial advice.
- Any claim about a different person who shares the name Seunghoon Choi or 최승훈.
